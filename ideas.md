# Diet Planner design brief

## Candidate directions

1. **Clinical dashboard meets fresh kitchen** — grounded, calm, data-forward wellness UI with warm food accents. Probability: 0.08.
2. **Midnight performance lab** — dark athletic analytics with electric highlights and dense charts. Probability: 0.07.
3. **Quiet pantry journal** — editorial recipe notebook with paper textures and handwritten warmth. Probability: 0.06.

## Committed direction: Clinical dashboard meets fresh kitchen

The product should feel trustworthy without pretending to be clinical care. The visual movement combines a calm dashboard with the warmth of a well-organized kitchen: generous paper-colored surfaces, clear data cards, and small bursts of fresh produce color.

- **Core principles:** calm, practical, optimistic, transparent, and easy to scan.
- **Color philosophy:** deep ink/navy for reading, warm paper for the canvas, leaf green for positive progress and primary actions, apricot for food and hydration highlights, and coral only for gentle warnings.
- **Layout paradigm:** responsive two-column dashboard on desktop, single-column stack on mobile; a consistent left rail/header shell; cards with clear hierarchy instead of decorative clutter.
- **Signature elements:** a plate-and-leaf mark, pill-shaped meal tags, thin progress bars, compact macro chips, and a “wellness example” label near generated content.
- **Interaction philosophy:** show immediate intent, explain what is happening, keep errors close to the field, and make the next action obvious.
- **Animation:** subtle 160–220ms fade/slide transitions only for cards and feedback; no looping motion or distracting parallax.
- **Typography system:** system sans stack for accessibility and performance; large editorial-style headings paired with compact uppercase labels.
- **Brand essence:** “Plan gently. Track clearly.”
- **Brand voice:** supportive, direct, transparent about approximations, and never medicalized.
- **Wordmark/logo:** Dietly Planner wordmark paired with a flat plate-and-leaf symbol.
- **Signature brand color:** leaf green `#2f7d5b`.

## Asset workflow

The application is functional/dashboard-oriented, so no hero photography or generic stock imagery is needed. The required project-specific logo is a small flat SVG with a warm paper background, a green plate, and an apricot leaf; the same mark is used for the favicon and app shell.
